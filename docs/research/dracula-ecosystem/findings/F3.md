### C4 — The Tailwind port is the ecosystem's real colour derivation: 11 families × 10 steps, hue-constant, HLS-lightness-linear, saturation free to fall.

`dracula/tailwind/colors.js` (HEAD `81eb383`, 288 lines). **99 distinct hexes outside our 29** — by far the largest single derivation in this angle.

**No generator exists.** `package.json` has no `scripts` beyond a failing test stub; there is no build step; the ramps are hand-authored literals. **There is no formula — the algorithm is characterised below by measurement, and that characterisation is inference, not documented.**

Structure: 22 keys, but only **11 distinct** — `nosferatu` (`:146-157`) is byte-identical to `darker` (`:3-14`), `aro`→`dark`, `cullen`→`light`, `vonCount`→`blue`, `vanHelsing`→`cyan`, `blade`→`green`, `morbius`→`orange`, `buffy`→`pink`, `dracula`→`purple`, `marcelin`→`red`, `lincoln`→`yellow`. The 11 Dracula-character aliases are duplicate ramps under theme names (`INSTALL.md:29-33` advertises `bg-buffy` / `text-nosferatu`).

Each family: steps `50…900` + `DEFAULT`, where `DEFAULT` is the canonical Dracula hex and it lands at **step 300** for chromatics (`purple.DEFAULT #bd93f9` == `purple[300]`) and at **step 900** for the two neutrals (`darker[900] #282a36` == Background, `dark[900] #44475a` == Comment).

**Measured ramp law** (computed from the literals, `colorsys.rgb_to_hls`):

| Family | H across 50→900 | S across 50→900 | L across 50→900 | L step |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `purple` | 265.0→264.6 (const) | 94.7→78.8 (**falls**) | 92.6→33.3 | 6.59 |
| `green` | 135.2→135.4 (const) | 100.0→85.8 (**falls**) | 94.3→27.7 | 7.41 |
| `darker` (neutral) | 232.2→231.4 (const) | 29.9→14.9 (**falls**) | 84.9→18.4 | **7.39** |
| `dark` (neutral) | 231.4→231.8 (const) | 28.0→13.9 (**falls**) | 97.7→31.0 | **7.41** |
| `blue` | 225.7→226.5 (const) | 35.6→20.8 (**falls**) | 88.4→29.2 | 6.58 |

So: **hue pinned, L stepped ~6.6–7.4 points, S allowed to decay with L.** That is the mirror image of our own 45 ramps, which pin S constant and let L vary (`hsl(264.71 89.47% 44%)` — note `264.71`/`89.47` are exactly `purple[300] #bd93f9`'s measured H and S). **Our ramps and the ecosystem ramp share the anchor hex and the hue, but disagree on which of H/S is held constant.**

**The neutrals are hue-matched to their own anchors**, which is the useful part: `darker` holds H≈231.2 (Background `#282a36` is H=231.4); `dark` holds H≈231.8 (Comment `#44475a` is H=231.8). Neutral ramps are therefore *tinted*, not grey.

**The two neutrals are the only surface-relevant output.** The 9 chromatic families are data/syntax scales, not surfaces. Non-29 neutral values:

- `darker` 50→800: `#CDD0E4 #B5BAD6 #9EA4C8 #888FB8 #727AA8 #5F6795 #53597C #454A64 #373A4D` — `colors.js:3-11`
- `dark` 50→800: `#F7F7FB #DFE1ED #C7CADF #B1B5D0 #9BA0C0 #858BB0 #71779F #606689 #525771` — `colors.js:16-24`

**Neither ramp crosses the body.** `darker` 900 = `#282a36` is the body itself; there is no step below it. The Tailwind port therefore has **no "below body" surface tier at all** — unlike dracula-css (C5) and Chrome (C3), which both reach below `#21222C`.
Confidence: **primary** (literals) for the values; **inference** for the law, since no generator or doc states it.

## Conflicting evidence

**X1 — Focus hue: `#815CD6` vs `#BD93F9`, inside one library.** `_forms.scss:56-60` and `_web-components.scss:128-132` style the same control. Forms uses `--drac-ui-focus` (#815CD6) with a 3px ring; web-components uses `--drac-purple` (#BD93F9) with a 2px ring. Both are in our 29. **Resolution: neither wins — this is a bug in the library, not a design disagreement.** The library defines a dedicated `ui.focus` token (`:44`, `tokens.ts:44`) and then does not use it in its own component layer. Confidence: primary.

**X2 — Hover direction is three-way across consumers.** dracula-css `_forms.scss:131` **lightens by hue swap** (purple→pink). dracula-css `_web-components.scss:223` **darkens below the surface** (listitem → `ui-bg-dark`). Obsidian `theme.css:48` **lightens with a white rim** (`rgba(255,255,255,0.09→0.16)`). Our kit darkens toward black (`#0000001F`). Three mechanisms, no shared doctrine. **This is the ecosystem having no answer, not having a different answer.**

**X3 — `ui-bg-dark` is *below* the base, `ui-bg-light` is *above*.** `_variables.scss:28-32` names confuse ordinal position with lightness. `#21222c` (Background Dark) is darker than the `$drac-bg: #282a36` base it is grouped with, while `#343746` (Current Line) is lighter. Two official consumers inherit the ambiguity. Any reader resolving `$drac-ui-bg-dark` as "a dark surface" will pick the wrong tier.

**X4 — Tailwind neutral ramps disagree with dracula-css on how far below body to go.** Tailwind's `darker[900]` *is* `#282a36`; there is no below-body tier. dracula-css has `#191a21` and Chrome has `#17181E`/`#191A21`. **Resolution: the drift is upward in specificity.** A CSS utility framework needs a named shade for `bg-darker`; an app theme targeting a specific product needs a concrete deeper frame. Neither is wrong; they answer different questions.

**X5 — `#191A21` job conflict (see C2).** Our `BRAND.md:16` says shadow-washes-only. `dracula-css/_forms.scss:63` and `google-chrome/manifest.json:15` both use it as a fill. **Resolution: the ecosystem evidence is for the *value*, which corroborates ours; the *job* is the disagreement, and ours is the narrower constraint.** This is a candidate to strengthen our existing rule, not a candidate to add.

**X6 — base16 `base00: 282936` vs our `#282A36`.** A first-party-org repo with a typo'd Background. Anyone importing `base16-dracula-scheme` gets a 1-byte-off body colour. Flag, do not adopt.
